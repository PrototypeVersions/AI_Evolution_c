The Long Run

One persistent world that is never reset. Agents carry small behaviour programs that mutate when they reproduce; they forage, breed, learn, move between four habitats, and can steal from neighbours. Nothing about their strategies is scripted. The world advances every day and this page shows what has happened so far.

Living strategies

A strategy shape is a program with its numeric thresholds blanked out; programs that differ only in tuning share a shape. The example is the most common exact program within the shape. Rules are checked top to bottom and the first match fires.
